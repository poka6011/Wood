    • The timber is produced in four grades: Grading is based on the worst side of the product, considering the quantity and size of defects in the wood:
    - Select (Grade 0) (or "Extra"/"Prima"): Highest quality, almost no knots, cracks, or resin pockets;
    - 1st grade: Minimal number of healthy, merged knots (usually up to 10 mm);
    - 2nd grade: Small cracks, healthy knots, resin pockets, slight blue stain are acceptable;
    - 3rd grade: Presence of missing knots, cracks, wormholes, sapwood;
    - 4th grade: Lowest grade, allowing significant defects (rot, a large amount of sapwood), but maintains the structural integrity.

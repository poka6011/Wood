Birch – a true symbol of Russia, belongs
to the broadleaf tree species.

The reason for its use is its good physical characteristics. Birch is a medium-density wood. It has good elasticity and impact strength. Birch timber is used in furniture manufacturing and interior finishing.

Properties:

  • most species have a white color with a slight yellowish tint.;
  • eco-friendly;
  • unique texture;
  • high wear resistance;
  • durability.

Applications:

  • not used for exterior finishing.;
  • solid birch makes excellent flooring;
  • solid, specially prepared wood is used to make individual parts of furniture constructions .

Processing birch boards with oils increases the product's lifespan, enhances its strength, and makes it more resistant to mechanical impacts. Wood treated in this way is easier to process and can be used to make carved elements for furniture. In construction, only plywood sheets are used.

Spruce – a coniferous tree species

Quite common throughout Russia. The accessibility of spruce is the reason for its popularity as a raw material for producing timber products. Spruce timber is widely used in construction and building renovation, making boards, block-house cladding, imitation timber, veneer, baseboards, and Euro-panels.

Key characteristics of spruce wood:

  • beautiful white color that lasts for a long time.;
  • slightly expressed texture, with fibers evenly arranged;
  • density at 12% moisture: 430 kg/m³;
  • unique timber pattern;
  • medium resin content and good resistance to rotting;
  • freshly cut wood has a low tendency to bluish discoloration;
  • the wood is durable but soft, resistant to warping;
  • one of the main features of spruce is its plasticity, making it easy to process by hand or machine;
  • non-toxic;
  • lightweight, with low density, meaning low thermal conductivity;
  • small diameter knots form an interesting pattern;
  • the unusual wood color creates a unique interior look, with light pink or golden shades.

Homogeneous texture gives the timber smoothness and evenness.

Cedar – a coniferous tree species.

Highly valued as a wood for deck construction. Others rely on it for furniture making, while some use it for carpentry crafts..

Key characteristics:

  • the surface has various shades, from nearly white to brown;
  • the texture looks and feels like a natural material, resistant to fading, and does not necessarily need to be painted;
  • durability is one of the reasons why many in construction choose this wood;
  • very resistant to decay, pest attacks (including rodents and various insects), and moisture absorption;
  • requires less maintenance and does not deform even in extreme conditions.

Strong color tones make it visually appealing for natural finishes. The wood has natural oils that protect it from the elements.

Larch – a coniferous tree species.

One of the most common valuable timber species used to produce timber products. It has unique characteristics. The main ones are high strength and long service life.

Experts highlight several advantages of larch, which determine its popularity and suitability for construction, furniture making, interior decoration, and other tasks.

Key characteristics:

  • aesthetic appearance, with a warm color and attractive pattern;
  • reliability and high strength, with a dense structure;
  • long service life;
  • moisture resistance: Larch produces resins and other compounds that provide natural protection against moisture and rotting.

Aspen – a broadleaf tree species.

The unique properties of aspen wood make it suitable for making strong household items or as a building material. Its boards have a white color with a slight greenish or bluish tint. Small yellowish spots repeat the core structure.

Main properties:

  • lightness.;
  • straight grain;
  • uniformity;
  • softness;
  • resistance to prolonged exposure to water.

Historically, aspen was used to make utensils. Houses were lined with aspen boards to create a fragrant indoor atmosphere.

Key features:

  • durability;
  • strength;
  • waterproofing;
  • resistance to decay;
  • uniform structure;
  • high density and fine fiber texture;
  • once dried and hardened, it becomes abrasion-resistant;
  • exhibits antimicrobial action due to phytoncides;
  • requires no additional finishing.

It is also known for emitting a silver-like light.

Pine – a coniferous tree species.

A centuries-old tree that can grow up to 600 years and reach a height of 75 meters. It thrives in open areas as it is a sun-loving plant and finds wide use in producing timber, building and finishing works, and furniture manufacturing. Pine surpasses broadleaf trees in strength and biological resistance while having lower production costs. Its trunks are generally straighter and have fewer defects.

Characteristics:

  • beautiful texture with a pleasant golden or reddish tint;
  • easy to process, timber made from it is eco-friendly and durable;
  • strength;
  • good thermal insulation properties;
  • easy to apply protective and decorative coatings;
  • elegant pattern;
  • beneficial aromatic scent of pine needles;
  • excellent soundproofing qualities;
  • outstanding moisture resistance;
  • medium density (at 12% moisture, density is 510 kg/m³);
  • resistant to decay and fungi.
